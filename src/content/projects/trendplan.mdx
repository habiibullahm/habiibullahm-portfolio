Weekly TikTok content planner for the Couple Date Ideas niche: auth, onboarding,
trend recommendations with caption and hashtag suggestions, copy-to-TikTok /
copy-week, a seven-day planner with drag-and-drop day moves, creating your own
idea on empty days, and progress tracking — shipped on Vercel with Neon Postgres.
Open Live for the public read-only `/demo` app tour (no login).
